# SALUS Project

Welcome to the **SALUS** project, an EU-funded initiative aimed at securing the Internet of Things (IoT) and advancing digital forensics for Law Enforcement Agencies (LEAs) by providing new investigation schemes and advanced IoT forensic tools for threat detection, evidence collection, and cross-agency collaboration.

## Project Scope

SALUS addresses the growing security challenges of IoT-enabled environments while bridging critical gaps in forensic investigation capabilities and legal frameworks. By combining a novel Digital-Twin (DT) infrastructure for proactive threat simulation, a secure Software-Defined-Network-as-a-Security-Service (SDaaSS) architecture, and AI-powered forensic capabilities, the project delivers tools designed for real-time IoT device detection, lawful evidence interception, and a blockchain-based chain of custody to ensure the integrity of digital evidence. Key objectives include:

- Developing a novel **SDaaSS** infrastructure to tackle cyber-physical security attacks in IoT-enabled environments.
- Designing new **IoT-oriented police investigation schemes** with lawful forensics analysis, in line with EU legal, ethical, and privacy values.
- Validating and demonstrating SALUS technologies in **five real-life pilot use cases** across police authorities and critical-infrastructure providers in the healthcare and energy sectors.
- Building a **pan-European cybersecurity collaboration framework** for outreach, market exploitation, and standardization.

SALUS results are validated in collaboration with five police authorities and two critical-infrastructure providers, supported by training activities, joint exercises, and operational hackathons to amplify adoption of the tools. This project is made possible with the collaboration of 19 partners from 13 countries — research organisations, technology providers, NGOs, and end-user police authorities — coordinated by the Hellenic Mediterranean University (HMU).
